Each project allows me to grow. For this project, I learned…
	•	that the design and CAD phase is very important, as it saves a lot of time later on when building.
	•	to 3D print test pieces to avoid parts that don’t fit.
	•	to place the pinouts on the microcontroller near where they will be connected, making it easier to troubleshoot.
	•	that it is important to ensure the motors are receiving enough current.
